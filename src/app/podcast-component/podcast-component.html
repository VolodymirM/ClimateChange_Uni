The evidence is clear, the warming is real

The Overwhelming Scientific Consensus

To comprehend the scale of this emergency, climatologists rely on comprehensive global data. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) Sixth Assessment Report provides unequivocal evidence that human activities have drastically warmed the atmosphere, ocean, and land. The report emphasizes that extreme weather
events — such as prolonged droughts, intense heatwaves, and erratic, destructive rainfall — are no longer future anomalies but current realities. This scientific consensus directly mirrors the severe weather scenarios, including the localized extreme shifts mentioned in our "2045 Future News" broadcast. The data confirms that without immediate and deep emissions reductions, these extreme events will only multiply in frequency and intensity.
